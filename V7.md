* **Validation ≥ 0.998 is impossible with the current candidates.** Even a perfect model stops at 0.99635.
  Blocking misses 1.2 % of true pairs and costs 0.0037. The model costs another 0.0051 (FN 22.3 k vs FP 1.4 k).
  Recall is still the loss on both sides.
* The leader's 0.99765 is above our ceiling, so they must have better candidate recall. Public repositories for
  this challenge reach at most 0.976 with 96.5 % candidate recall, and the leaders haven't published.
* **Test vs validation:** US and India behave on test as on DMS (test/DMS predicted-per-S1 ratio 1.0019 / 0.996).
  If they score ≈ 0.991 on test, the LB 0.9881 implies **France ≈ 0.97**, about 0.003 of LB. That's an
  inference, and the V7 diagnostics measure it without labels.

tensors, reloading, and out-of-fold re-scoring (identical to the training-time scores).

## 4. Decision rules (fixed now, `code/v7/cands_plan.py`, `code/v7/choose_v7.py`)

**Candidate passes (`cands_plan.py`):**
* Deeper lists only if they add ≥ 0.0005 recall within the pair budget.
* A block pass takes its largest cap that adds ≥ 0.0002 recall at ≤ 1.5 pairs per record.
* A sibling pass is kept if it adds ≥ 0.0002 recall.
* Over the budget, the least efficient passes are dropped. Test uses exactly the passes train kept.

**Plan and pick (`choose_v7.py`):**
* Round 2 unless round 1 is better on DMS. Caps unless they cost more than 0.0001.
* EM, France gamma and pseudo-labels are taken from the V6 plan.
* **Submit V7** if DMS(V7) ≥ DMS(V6) + 0.0005 and the US/India consistency check passes (V6 rule).
* Order: `final_v7` > `final_v6` > `final_v3nognn` > V2.
* Both scores count every true pair (also those blocking missed), so higher candidate recall shows up directly.

## 5. Honest expectations

* **The ceiling is the first number to watch** (`results/v7/v7_plan.json`, `ceiling_v7`). If the passes lift DMS
  pair recall from 0.988 to ~0.996, the ceiling moves to ~0.999. At V6's model efficiency that's worth roughly
  +0.002 to +0.003 on validation (≈ 0.9935–0.994).
* **Validation 0.998+ also needs the scorer gap (0.005) mostly closed.** That's where the FN sample and the stronger
  rerankers come in: V8, driven by the diagnostics.
* **LB 0.997 additionally needs France fixed** (≈ 0.003 if the inference in §0 holds).

I won't promise 0.997. V7 removes the one limit that made it impossible, and the diagnostics show exactly what's
left.
